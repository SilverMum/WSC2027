CONSERVATION

PROTECTING OUR NATURAL HERITAGE

Conservation Advocacy

Protecting Ontario's Fish, Wildlife, and Natural Habitats

The OFAH plays a pivotal role in conservation advocacy across Ontario, spearheading initiatives that positively impact the province's diverse ecosystems, fish and wildlife populations, and the habitats that support them.

Conservation Projects

The OFAH engages in habitat restoration and enhancement, scientific research, and advocacy for policy reforms.

Examples include work alongside the Central Lake Ontario Conservation Authority (CLOCA) to enhance wetland habitats critical for waterfowl and other wildlife species.

OFAH also works with organizations such as the Couchiching Conservancy to protect and restore important habitats.

Together, these collaborations promote responsible stewardship of Ontario's natural resources.

Advocacy & Education

The OFAH advocates for evidence-based decision-making that prioritizes conservation and the sustainable management of Ontario's natural resources.

Its education and outreach programs empower Ontarians to participate in conservation efforts and help cultivate a community committed to environmental stewardship.
